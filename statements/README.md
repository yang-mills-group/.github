# Statements and rules

This directory preserves unchanged copies of the Yang–Mills Working Group's statements and rules published on the [KITP website](https://www.kitp.ucsb.edu/statement-by-the-yangmills-working-group). The copies were downloaded on **8 November 2026**. They record the group's statements at the time they were issued; later assessments and releases are listed in the [organization overview](../README.md).

| Document | Date printed in the document | Archived copy | KITP source |
|---|---|---|---|
| First statement by the shepherding committee | October 2026 | [PDF](2026-10-statement.pdf) | [Original PDF](https://www.kitp.ucsb.edu/sites/default/files/YM_working_group.pdf) |
| Second statement by the shepherding committee | 3 November 2026 | [PDF](2026-11-03-statement.pdf) | [Original PDF](https://www.kitp.ucsb.edu/sites/default/files/2nd-Statement-About-Quantum-YangMills-Theory_Nov-3.pdf) |
| Rules and Procedures of the Yang Mills Group | Undated | [PDF](rules-and-procedures-archived-2026-11-08.pdf) | [Original PDF](https://www.kitp.ucsb.edu/sites/default/files/RULES%20AND%20PROCEDURES%20OF%20THE%20YANG%20MILLS%20GROUP.pdf) |

The first statement gives a month but no day. The date in the rules filename is the archive date, not an adoption or publication date. The [source record](sources.json) includes the download URLs, retrieval times, file sizes, and SHA-256 checksums. Later statements or revisions should be added as new files so that these published versions remain available.

These PDFs retain their original text and attribution. Archiving them here does not change their licensing or place them under the repository's Apache-2.0 license.
